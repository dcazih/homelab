QUESTIONS?

Good to know.

What formats does Riplet support?+

Free conversions include MP3, MP4, M4A and WebM, with audio up to 128 kbps, video up to 720p, 4-hour audio and 1.5-hour video. Supporters also get WAV, audio up to 320 kbps, video up to 4K, 12-hour audio and 4-hour video.

Which audio quality should I choose?+

128 kbps is compact and good for spoken audio. Supporters can select 192 or 320 kbps, though that cannot improve the original source quality.

Does Riplet work on my phone?+

The website works on modern phones, tablets, and computers. Safari may require an extra tap to save a finished download.

Why did my conversion fail?+

A URL may be unavailable, restricted or unsupported. Temporary service issues can also prevent a conversion.
