Privacy at Riplet

Riplet processes the video URL and format you submit to create a temporary download. The converter runs on our server. A finished file is deleted after its download or after about five minutes if it is not downloaded. Failed job files are removed sooner. IP-based request counts are kept in server memory for rate limiting for up to an hour.

Supporter keys and payments

Ko-fi processes payments. After a qualifying payment, its webhook sends Riplet the payment email, amount and transaction identifier. Riplet stores the email, transaction identifier, key hash and expiration date to issue and validate access. Riplet sends the activation email through Resend. The key is stored in your browser until it expires or you clear site data.

Browser storage

Riplet stores your theme preference and, if you activate supporter access, your key in your browser’s local storage. The site does not run ads. It loads fonts from Google Fonts.

Questions

For a privacy or supporter-key question, contact Riplet through its Ko-fi page.
