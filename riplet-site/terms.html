Using Riplet

Riplet converts supported links into downloadable audio or video files. Only convert material you are allowed to download and use. You are responsible for following the source platform’s rules and applicable rights.

Free and supporter access

Free access includes audio up to four hours at 128 kbps and video up to 1.5 hours at 720p. A qualifying $5 USD Ko-fi payment gives 15 days of supporter access from payment time: audio up to 12 hours and 320 kbps, video up to four hours and 4K, and WAV output. The key is emailed to the payment email address and works in the browser where you activate it. Another qualifying payment grants a new 15-day key.

Availability

Conversion depends on the source video being available and accessible. Live, upcoming, restricted or unavailable videos may fail. Riplet may limit request volume or file sizes to keep the service running. A selected output resolution or bitrate cannot add quality missing from the source.

Help

See the help page for download steps and limits. For a payment or key issue, contact Riplet through Ko-fi.
