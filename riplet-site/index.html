HELP & ANSWERS

Good to know.

Quality, formats and file sizes explained before you download.

How do I convert a YouTube link?+

Copy the URL of a supported video or Short, paste it above, select MP3, MP4 or supporter WAV when available, then tap Rip! The download starts when conversion finishes. You can also read the conversion guide.

What formats does Riplet support?+

Free conversions include MP3 and MP4, with audio up to 192 kbps and video up to 1080p. Supporters also get WAV, audio up to 320 kbps and video up to 4K.

Which audio quality should I choose?+

Use 128 kbps for speech and smaller files. The supporter option of 320 kbps uses more space and may help music if the source quality supports it. WAV is lossless PCM output, useful for editing, but converting a lossy source to WAV cannot restore missing detail. WAV files are much larger and their size depends on sample rate and channels.

Which MP4 quality should I use?+

720p is a practical choice for phones and smaller downloads. 1080p looks sharper on larger screens. 4K needs a 4K source and substantially more storage; 4K requires supporter access. If the source is lower resolution, choosing a higher output setting cannot add detail.

Does Riplet work on my phone?+

Riplet works in modern phone, tablet and desktop browsers. On iPhone, Safari may ask permission to paste from your clipboard or need another tap to save a completed download.

Why did my conversion fail?+

The video may be unavailable, restricted, live, longer than your plan allows, or temporarily blocked by the source. Check the URL and try again. Free limits are 4 hours for audio and 1.5 hours for video; supporter limits are 12 hours and 4 hours.
