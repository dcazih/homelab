Riplet help and conversion guide

How to use Riplet

  1. Paste a supported YouTube video or Shorts URL.
  2. Choose MP3, MP4 or supporter WAV and select an available quality.
  3. Tap Rip! and wait for the file to finish. Your browser starts the download.

Free and supporter limits

Free: 4-hour audio, 1.5-hour video, up to 192 kbps audio and 1080p video. Supporter: 12-hour audio, 4-hour video, up to 320 kbps audio, 4K video and WAV. A $5 Ko-fi payment gives 15 days of supporter access after your emailed key is activated.

Mobile downloads

On iPhone, allow the browser to paste when prompted. If Safari does not automatically save a finished file, tap the download link again. Check your browser’s Downloads folder.

Conversion errors

Check the link, video availability and duration. Live and upcoming videos are unsupported. Temporary source restrictions or busy service periods can also prevent conversion.
